# Project Brief

## Problem

Planning a trip often spreads decisions across chats, maps, calendars, notes, booking sites, and budget trackers. Travel Assistant should bring these pieces into one guided planning flow.

## Target Users

- Solo travelers who want faster planning.
- Couples, families, and small groups coordinating preferences.
- Frequent travelers reusing saved preferences and past trip patterns.

## Core Scenarios

- Create a trip from destination, dates, budget, and travel style.
- Compare itinerary options and tradeoffs.
- Save places, reservations, notes, and reminders.
- Export or share a trip plan.

## Early Product Questions

- Which platform comes first: web, mobile, chat-first, or API-first?
- Which integrations are essential: maps, flights, hotels, calendars, weather, documents?
- What data should be stored locally vs. synced?
- What level of AI autonomy is acceptable for booking-related flows?
